Privacy Policy

Buffer is a co-parenting communication tool in private beta, operated by {{ brand }}. This policy covers the Buffer website and the text messaging number {{ number }}.

What is collected

  • If you ask for beta access: your name, email address, mobile number, and the date, time and wording of your agreement to receive text messages.
  • If you text or call a Buffer number: your phone number, the content of your text and picture messages, the date and time, and delivery status reported by phone carriers. Calls are not answered and are not recorded; only the calling number and time are noted.

How it is used

  • To consider and answer requests for beta access.
  • To deliver messages between a Buffer user and their co-parent.
  • To keep an accurate, unaltered record of that communication.
  • To organize and summarize incoming messages for the Buffer user, using automated software.
  • To notify the Buffer user by text when a message or call arrives.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information passes through service providers that operate the phone numbers and the software: a messaging provider, a hosting provider, and a provider of automated text processing. They handle it only to provide those services.

A Buffer user controls the record of their own conversation and may give it to attorneys, a court, or others where the law requires or permits it in a family-law matter.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept as a permanent record and are stored encrypted. Beta access requests are kept until the beta ends or you ask for yours to be removed.

Your choices

Reply STOP to a Buffer number at any time to stop receiving texts from it. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. To have a beta access request removed, text {{ number }}.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by text message to {{ number }}.
